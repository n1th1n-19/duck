Duck Share shares files and messages between nearby devices over your local Wi-Fi network.

Hold two phones together to send, like AirDrop: tap to share works over NFC, even when Duck Share is closed on both phones. Share from any app with Share → Duck Share, and the other phone saves the file in the background.

- No internet, no account, no ads, no tracking*
- End-to-end encrypted with HTTPS
- Trusted devices send without asking; block devices you don't want to hear from
- Works with LocalSend on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android

Duck Share is a fork of LocalSend (Apache 2.0).

*The operating system may still gather usage data.
