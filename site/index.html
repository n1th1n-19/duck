Hold two phones together.
The file goes across.

Duck Share sends files and messages to nearby devices over your local network. No internet, no account, no cloud. On Android it works like AirDrop, even when the app is closed.

Download for Android Source code

Free and open source · Android 7.0 or newer · Pick arm64-v8a if unsure

Tap to share

Hold the backs of two phones together. NFC does the handshake, Wi-Fi carries the file. No pairing, no picking from a list.

Works with the app closed

Share from any app with Share → Duck Share. The other phone wakes up from the tap and saves the file in the background.

Private by design

Files go straight from device to device, encrypted. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is collected. Trust or block devices you know.

Works with LocalSend

Duck Share speaks the LocalSend protocol, so it sends to and receives from LocalSend on Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS and Android.

How tap to share works

  1. In any app, tap Share → Duck Share, or pick files in Duck Share.
  2. Hold the phones back to back. Duck Share shows where the NFC spot is on your phone.
  3. Both phones buzz. Pull them apart: the file goes over Wi-Fi, and the other phone gets a notification that opens it.

Both phones need NFC and the same Wi-Fi. On Pixel phones, turn off Google's own Tap to Share in the Quick Share settings.

Play Protect warning? Duck Share is a new independent app, so Android may warn before installing it. Tap More details → Install anyway. Every release is signed with the same Duck Share key.
