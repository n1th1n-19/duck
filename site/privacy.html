Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 October 2026

Duck Share is a free, open-source app for sending files and messages to nearby devices. It is developed by n1th1n-19 and based on LocalSend.

In short: Duck Share does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no servers of ours.

What happens to your files

Files and messages go directly from your device to the device you send them to, over your local network. They are encrypted in transit (HTTPS with a certificate generated on your device). They are never uploaded to the internet or to anyone else.

Received files are saved where you choose (by default your Downloads folder or gallery). Duck Share keeps a history of received files on your device; you can delete it at any time in the app.

What other devices see

To be found and to receive files, Duck Share shares a few details with devices on the same network, and with a phone you tap:

  • the device name and device type you set in the app,
  • the device's local network addresses and the app's port,
  • a fingerprint of the app's security certificate.

These details are needed to make a connection. They are not sent anywhere else.

Tap to share (NFC)

When you hold two phones together, they exchange the details listed above over NFC. The file itself then travels over your local network. Nothing is sent to us or to a third party.

Internet

Duck Share works without an internet connection and does not contact any server on its own. It only opens a website when you tap a link in the app, such as this policy or the source code. This website loads no trackers, analytics or third-party fonts.
